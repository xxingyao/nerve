    Demo settings and real hardware

    To switch a real lamp or fan in the prototype, flash a smart plug with Tasmota (or use a Shelly plug), put it on the same Wi-Fi as this laptop, open index.html from disk, and enter the plug's IP. NERVE will switch it whenever it switches Workstations A. This does not work from the hosted link, because browsers block plain-HTTP calls from HTTPS pages.

    What is simulated, and the assumptions behind the savings
    • Everything on this page is a simulation of one small office (four zones, up to 18 people). Headcount follows a typical weekday; weekends are empty.
    • Business as usual runs the same people through an identical office with cooling on a fixed 7:30 to 18:30 schedule and desk power on all day. It runs three times: careful (24°C setpoint, 10% of desks left on overnight), typical (23°C, 30%) and careless (22.5°C, 50%). Savings are shown as a range across the three.
    • Occupancy uses motion (PIR/mmWave) plus a count of Wi-Fi devices. No cameras. Wi-Fi alone only counts as presence for 10 minutes after the last motion, so a laptop left docked overnight does not keep the lights on.
    • Room temperature and cooling power use a simple heat-balance model. Real numbers need a pilot: measure one floor for two weeks without NERVE, then two weeks with it.
    • Comfort is the share of occupied minutes at or below 26°C. NERVE cools to 24.5°C, goes to full cooling above 26°C, and any occupant can ask for 1°C cooler for an hour.
